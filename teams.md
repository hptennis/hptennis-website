The club runs Ladies' and Men's teams competing in the [Tennis Sussex Leagues](https://www.tennissussex.com/the-tennis-sussex-leagues-a-summary/).

Home matches are normally played on Sundays, generally starting at 10:00 – 10:30. Saturday match times will be advised. Home match court bookings are made in ClubSpark and take priority over all other bookings, so courts can be hard to get on match mornings.
