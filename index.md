## About the club

Hampden Park Tennis Club is in the delightful area of Hampden Park in Eastbourne, surrounded by Decoy Wood and Ham Shaw where a variety of wildlife abounds. We have five superb floodlit astroturf courts, and a clubhouse with kitchen facilities and seating for visiting teams and social events.

We're a friendly club with regular [club sessions](/sessions/) and plenty of competitive tennis. We run Men's and Ladies' summer and winter [teams](/matches/) in the Sussex Tennis Leagues, with our 1st teams in Division 1. We also offer professional [coaching](/coaching/) and have a very active junior section.
