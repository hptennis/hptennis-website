## Artificial Clay Sweeping & Maintenance Schedule

You should drag-sweep an artificial clay court **after every match or playing session**. This takes about **3 to 5 minutes** and keeps an even **2 mm layer** of loose clay infill over the underlying stability carpet.

### Recommended Schedule

* **After Every Session (3–5 minutes):** Drag the court in an inward spiral or elliptical pattern using a drag mat or combination drag brush. Include the run-off areas behind the baseline to pull displaced infill back into high-wear zones.
* **After Dragging:** Use a handheld line brush to sweep the infill off the court lines so they stay clearly visible for the next players.
* **Weekly:** Clear leaves, twigs, and debris from the surface and perimeter. Do a thorough drag-brush from the outer edges inward to return any infill that has drifted toward the fencing.
* **Monthly or Bi-Monthly:** Check infill levels across the baseline and service areas, adding small top-ups where the carpet fibres begin to show.
* **1–2 Times Per Year:** Arrange a professional deep clean to decompact the infill, extract embedded ball fluff or moss, and redistribute the surface evenly.

### Important Rule for Wet Weather

* **Never sweep a wet court:** Dragging when the surface is wet or damp causes the clay infill to clump into lumps and damages the carpet pile. Always wait until the surface is dry before brushing.
