## Astroturf (Synthetic Grass) Maintenance Schedule

You should drag-brush an astroturf tennis court **once a week, or roughly every 10 hours of play**. Regular brushing prevents the sand infill from compacting, keeps the synthetic fibres standing upright, and preserves drainage.

### Recommended Schedule

* **Before or After Play (As Needed):** Clear loose leaves, twigs, pine needles, and court debris using a leaf blower or wide plastic rake. Organic matter left on the surface breaks down into silt, which blocks drainage and encourages moss.
* **Weekly / Every ~10 Hours of Play:** Drag-brush the court when completely dry using a soft-to-medium synthetic drag brush. Brush in varying directions (lengthways, widthways, and diagonally) to redistribute silica sand evenly and stop the carpet pile from flattening in one direction.
* **Monthly:** Inspect high-wear zones—especially the baseline and service boxes—for low sand levels. Top up with dry, rounded silica sand as needed so the carpet fibres remain supported.
* **Spring & Autumn (2 Times Per Year):** Apply a specialist, non-corrosive moss and algae treatment to prevent slippery green growth, particularly in shaded or damp corners.
* **Annually:** Book a professional deep-clean and decompaction service to extract fine dirt, ball fluff, and compacted sand, then replenish the infill.

### Important Rules for Care

* **Only brush when dry:** Brushing damp or wet sand causes it to clump and stick to the bristles rather than redistributing smoothly.
* **Avoid harsh chemicals and power washers:** Never use bleach, salt, or high-pressure jet washers, as these can damage the synthetic fibres, degrade the backing glue, and blast the sand infill out of the carpet.
* **Enforce proper footwear:** Require clean, non-marking tennis shoes to avoid bringing mud, grit, or clay onto the turf.
