# Updating the club website

You don't need any technical knowledge to update the website. You edit it in your web browser using **Pages CMS**, and your changes appear on the live site a minute or two after you save.

## Getting started (one-off)

1. Create a free GitHub account at <https://github.com/signup> if you don't have one.
2. Ask the website administrator to add you to the club's website. You'll get an email invitation. Accept it.
3. Go to <https://app.pagescms.org>, choose **Sign in with GitHub**, and pick **hptennis-website**.

## Making a change

1. Pick what you want to change from the menu on the left (for example **What's On** or **Membership fees**).
2. Edit the text in the boxes.
3. Click **Save**. The live site updates within about two minutes. Refresh the page to see it.

## Common jobs

| I want to... | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Add, change or hide a notice on the home page | **What's On**. Untick *Show on the home page* to hide one without deleting it. |
| Update upcoming events | **Events**. Use *Heading 3* for each event name. |
| Change session times | **Club Sessions**. Add, remove or drag rows in the lists. |
| Change membership prices | **Membership fees**. Update *Fees last updated* too. |
| Add or remove a coach | **Coaches** |
| Change the offer box or banner on the home page | **Home page** |
| Change an email address or ClubSpark link | **Club details** |

## Adding photos

In any **Photo** box, click to upload a picture from your computer or phone. Please keep photos under about 1MB. Photos straight from a phone are often 3–5MB, so shrink them first, for example with <https://squoosh.app>.

## Membership and court booking

These are managed in **ClubSpark**, not on this website. The site just links to it.

## Something went wrong?

Every change is saved in the history, so nothing is ever lost. Contact the website administrator and they can undo any change.
